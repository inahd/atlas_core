# Aboriginal Cosmological Overlay

## Objective
Investigate structural parallels between Australian Aboriginal cosmology (Dreamtime/Tjukurpa) and the Vedic cosmological framework used in Atlas, seeking genuine correspondences rather than forced mappings.

## Research Questions
1. What are the core spatial-temporal structures in Aboriginal cosmology (songlines, dreaming tracks, totemic geography)?
2. How does the Aboriginal concept of "country" as living body parallel the Vedic Vastu Purusha?
3. Are there structural (not cultural) parallels between songlines and nadi systems?
4. What ethical frameworks exist for cross-cultural cosmological comparison without appropriation?
