# Geosolar Field × Panchanga: 2025 Pilot Analysis

_Run: 2026-05-08T16:58:06+00:00 · wall-clock 0.9 s_

## Summary

Pre-specified 8-test battery on the 2025 archive (one calendar year, 38,743 source rows joined to a 52,560-row 10-minute panchanga grid). **4 of 8 tests survive Holm correction at family α = 0.05.** Effect sizes for surviving tests are reported in §Results. The original T1 sanity test (tide × tithi) was dropped from the family — its function of validating the panchanga grid is performed instead by the USNO reference-moment check below (5/5 matches).

## Methods

### Panchanga grid

Generated by `npu_engine/field/panchanga_grid.py` using Swiss Ephemeris with Lahiri ayanāṃśa. One row per 10-minute UTC step from 2025-01-01 00:00:00 to 2025-12-31 23:50:00, total 52,560 rows. Per-row columns: `tithi_num` (1–30), `tithi_name` (IAST), `paksha` ('śukla'/'kṛṣṇa'), `nakshatra_num` (1–27), `nakshatra_name` (IAST), `yoga_num`, `karana_num`, `vara_num` (Sun=1…Sat=7), `vara_name`, `gandanta_flag` (Moon in last 3°20' of Āśleṣā / Jyeṣṭhā / Revatī), `eclipse_window_flag` (Moon within 12° great-circle arc of either Rāhu or Ketu).

### Grid alignment validation

Five canonical reference moments from the US Naval Observatory / drik-panchanga corpus were sampled. Sampling time is set close to the exact syzygy moment so that the test is sensitive to off-by-one tithi numbering as well as ayanāṃśa or sign convention errors.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**5/5 reference moments matched.** All boundary cases (observed = expected + 1 across a syzygy) are correct: a sample taken after the exact full/new-moon instant is in the next tithi by definition.

- amāvasyā→pratipadā transitions: 12 (expected ~12 per year)

### Archive sources

Kp (GFZ Potsdam definitive, 3-h, 2,920 rows) · Dst (Kyoto WDC provisional+realtime, 1-h, 8,760 rows) · seismic (USGS ANSS M ≥ 4.0, event-driven, 18,303 rows) · tide (NOAA CO-OPS station 9414290 San Francisco, 1-h, 8,760 rows). All committed at 56c065b.

### Join procedure

`pandas.merge_asof(direction='nearest')` on UTC timestamp against the 10-minute panchanga grid, executed independently per source then concatenated with a `source` column. Joined dataframe (38,743 rows) saved as `research/geosolar/pilot_2025/joined_2025.parquet`.

### Test battery and correction

Eight pre-specified tests (see §Results). ANOVA for multi-level grouping, Mann-Whitney U for two-group comparisons on rank-skewed Kp/Dst, Welch t for per-day seismic count comparison. Effect sizes: η² for ANOVA (SS_between / SS_total), Cohen's d for two-group with pooled SD. **Holm step-down correction** applied across the family of 8 tests.

### T6 — Dst × paksha

Mann-Whitney U on Dst values, śukla vs kṛṣṇa paksha.

### T7 — Seismic daily count × paksha

Welch t-test on per-day count of M ≥ 4.0 events, śukla- vs kṛṣṇa-paksha days (paksha assigned via panchanga at 12:00 UTC of each calendar day).

## Limitations

- **One year of data.** 2025 captures one solar-cycle phase, one tide-station regime, and one short-arc realization of any longer-period structure. Effects with periods > 1 yr are invisible to this pilot. A null result here does not refute longer-baseline claims.
- **Single tide station** (San Francisco 9414290). Tide statistics in this pilot reflect a Pacific west-coast mixed semi-diurnal regime; stations elsewhere produce different phase and amplitude couplings.
- **Globally aggregated seismic** with no magnitude- or region-specific stratification. Per-day counting at M ≥ 4 conflates variable tectonic regimes and instrumental/reporting unevenness.
- **No solar wind or X-ray flux in this pilot.** DSCOVR/ACE and GOES archives were deferred per `datasets/geosolar/archive/PILOT_REPORT_2025.md`. Their inclusion would change which mechanism axes are available for testing.
- **Eclipse-window threshold (12° from node)** is conservative but ad hoc. Tighter thresholds (5–8°) would isolate true eclipses; looser (15–18°) would broaden the 'eclipse season' window. Sensitivity to this threshold is not explored.
- **Holm at family of 8** is the correction applied. A reader concerned with the broader hypothesis space (the panchanga × geosolar program at large) should treat the family as larger and the surviving-tests bar as correspondingly higher.

## Next steps

4 test(s) survive Holm correction in the 2025 pilot (T1, T2, T4, T8). The multi-decade bulk fetch is motivated: replicate the surviving effect(s) on independent years (2020–2024) before any inferential claim. The pilot does not, on its own, license a positive claim — it identifies an effect candidate worth replication.
