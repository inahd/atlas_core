Interpretation:
  Higher band alignment scores indicate more directional
  organization of vanadium-rich regions, which would correspond
  to clearer Damascus pattern in the final forged blade.

  Standard deviation of vanadium concentration in the solid
  indicates the degree of segregation - higher std = more
  pronounced banding.

Caveats:
  - This is plausibility modeling, not verified physics.
  - Real-world coupling magnitudes between geomagnetic field
    and steel solidification dendrite alignment are uncertain.
  - Validation requires physical experiments with controlled
    field conditions during high-carbon steel solidification.

Atlas integration:

  computed by Atlas's existing engines. The transduction layer
  combines them into the effective_field_strength used by
  the simulation. Outputs feed back into Atlas's relational
  graph as predicted-outcome data.
